## 5. Competitor / SERP observations

Based on searches 2026-11-04 (company sites, price-guide sites). Competitors seen: Hellamaid (heavy price-guide blog), Molly Maid, HomeStars price guides, Anago (commercial franchise), Transparent Cleaning (strata caretaking), many thin local pages.

- **Price queries are won by guides with tables.** Hellamaid publishes one guide per intent ("how much does deep cleaning / maid service / move-out cost in Vancouver") plus a price index. HomeStars and Angi/Fixr own cost queries elsewhere. Format: H1 question, summary box, range table by home size, factor list, FAQ.
- **Service queries are won by service pages** with a quote CTA, area strip, checklist and FAQ. Hellamaid also has per-suburb pages (e.g. West Vancouver). Competitors lean on "from $X" and "book in 60 seconds" style copy; compare ours with real numbers only.
- **Checklists** (room-by-room, printable) rank for informational queries and feed featured snippets. Our existing checklists are a good base.
- **Move-out SERPs** blend three groups: cleaning companies, government/tenant-rights pages (gov.bc.ca, Tenant Resource Centre, People's Law School) and news about deposit disputes. To win, mirror that structure: cite gov.bc.ca explicitly and keep legal claims modest.
- **Strata:** weak local competition; results skew Australian. Transparent Cleaning is the clear BC specialist. Opportunity for a clear BC-worded page.
- **Post-construction:** results polluted by Vancouver, WA price guides. Local intent must be explicit in headings and schema. No credible Vancouver BC price figure exists in the results: avoid quoting one.
- **Marketplace/job noise:** Indeed, BCAA marketplace and Bark appear for "house cleaner Vancouver"; they signal a "hire a person" intent. Our differentiator is the insured, vetted company (approved claims).
- **AI answers** tend to quote cost ranges from price guides and a few brand names. To be quoted: a table with our own prices, short answer blocks, and the same entity facts everywhere (already in `llms.txt`).
- **Quality warning:** several competitor "2026 price index" claims cite unverifiable sources. Do not copy their numbers.

## 6. Gaps and risks

1. **No volume data.** Everything above is qualitative. Check Search Console after launch and Keyword Planner before the long posts. Use the first 60 days of queries to re-rank the backlog.
2. **Vancouver BC vs Vancouver WA.** Always include "BC" / "Greater Vancouver" in H1, title and the first paragraph, and set `addressRegion` BC in schema (already). Expect misdirected impressions for "cleaning services Vancouver" in Washington.
3. **Unconfirmed claims to ask the client before copy ships:** whether we offer Airbnb linen/restock, inside-appliance cleaning in deep cleans, post-construction scope (drywall dust, windows, floors), strata work (any existing strata clients, WCB clearance letter), office sectors (medical/dental), carpet cleaning, hours (never claim after-hours), and a "free quote" promise.
4. **Local pack depends on Google Business Profile**, which is client-owned. Without GBP and reviews, "near me" queries will not convert; area pages only help organic results.
5. **Suburbs** (Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, etc.) hold the biggest demand but cannot be published without client confirmation (SEO-PLAN rule). The biggest single keyword upside is blocked on this.
6. **Legal topics** (RTB deposits, strata law, Airbnb licensing) must cite gov.bc.ca / vancouver.ca / the Strata Property Act, carry a "not legal advice" line, and be re-checked at publication. Fee figures conflicted across sources (e.g. Vancouver STR licence $1,108 vs others; RTB filing fee), so do not state fees without reading the current official page.
7. **Cannibalisation:** `/home-cleaning` currently targets deep, move-out and Airbnb terms in its supporting list. After the new pages ship, remove those from its meta/H2s and link out instead. Also keep `/custodian-services` as the commercial hub and `/office-cleaning` as the specific page: differentiate titles (janitorial vs office).
8. **Existing posts overlap** with new pages (move-out checklist -> `/move-out-cleaning`; deep vs regular -> `/deep-cleaning`; office checklist -> `/office-cleaning`; post-construction guide -> `/post-construction-cleaning`). Add reciprocal links; do not rewrite their targets to the money keywords.
9. **Thin brand signal:** "Akumal Executive Cleaning" searches may still go to the old listing/site. Keep the former-name sentence on Home, About and in schema `alternateName` (done).
10. **Titles:** the title template adds ` | S&A Cleaning Group` (21 chars). Keep page titles at or under 60 characters before that, per the brief. Some titles above run longer than ideal in rendered SERPs; trim if Search Console shows truncation.
11. **Reviews:** `AggregateRating` is not allowed until real reviews are approved for display. Do not add review stars or "5-star" claims to meta descriptions beyond the approved wording.
